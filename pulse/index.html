Macroeconomics

Week in a nutshell. Inflation is back. The Fed held at 3.75–4.00% on 28 Oct, but it was a "hawkish pause": markets now price roughly a 75% chance of a December hike. The ECB also held at 2.50% with a December hike widely expected, and Lagarde announced she will step down early. US tech stocks sold off hard on 30 Oct. Closer to home, the RBNZ kept the OCR on hold, and NZ economist Michael Reddell questioned how much attention it is paying to oil rather than core inflation.
