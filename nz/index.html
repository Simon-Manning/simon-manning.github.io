NZ Economy Explorer

About ten years of New Zealand macro data (2015 to the latest release), taken only from official sources: Stats NZ, the Reserve Bank (RBNZ) and Immigration NZ. Solid lines are published data. The dashed lines in the shaded area run to 2030 and are driven by the dials.

Projections are assumptions, not forecasts. Each dashed line simply carries the latest published value forward along the path you set with the dials. They are not predictions by me or by any agency, and nothing here is financial advice.

Dials (my assumptions to 2030)

The shaded area marks projections, from the latest data point to the end of 2030. "Glide" dials move the series in a straight line from the latest value to the level you set, reached after one year, then hold it flat. "Per year" dials compound or trend from the latest value.

What your dials imply

The imported-goods effect is a rough rule of thumb: about 25% of household spending goes on imported goods and services, multiplied by the % fall in the NZD, assuming full pass-through within the year. A weaker NZD adds to prices and a stronger one subtracts. It sits on top of the CPI dial, as if your CPI dial excluded currency moves. This is a simplification, not a Stats NZ measure.
